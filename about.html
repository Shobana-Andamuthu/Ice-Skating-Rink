Where Passion Meets the Ice

Our Heritage

Built for Skaters, Powered by Innovation

Frostiva was born in 2012 with a bold vision: to transform public ice skating from an ordinary pastime into a captivating, multi-sensory winter experience.

What started as a single community rink has expanded into a 45,000 sq.ft state-of-the-art dual ice complex featuring NHL regulation dimensions, Olympic figure skating training pads, and an immersive sound & light dome.

Arctic Tech

World-Class Rink Engineering

We utilize sub-zero laser thermal monitoring and zero-emission CO2 chillers for the smoothest glide in the country.

Laser-Leveled Real Ice

Automated Zamboni fleet equipped with micro-laser depth sensors maintain a flawless 1.25-inch ice sheet every 90 minutes.

100% Eco Green Cooling

Our closed-loop CO2 refrigeration captures reclaimed thermal energy to heat our spectator lounges, showers, and café warm zones.

Concert & Laser Dome

Sub-zero acoustic array with custom ceiling baffles deliver crisp 360-degree sound with synchronized atmospheric lighting effects.

Medical Grade Air

HEPA-13 air purifiers exchange arena air 8 times per hour, eliminating fog and maintaining pure, crisp mountain-quality oxygen.

Community First

Making the Ice Accessible to Everyone

We believe ice skating empowers physical health, discipline, and joy. Through our *Frostiva Ice for All* initiative, we partner with schools, charities, and adaptive sports organizations.

  • Over 5,000 free school field trip admissions every year
  • Sled Hockey equipment and dedicated adaptive ice slots
  • Need-based scholarships for aspiring young figure skaters
  • Senior morning social skate clubs with complimentary coffee
