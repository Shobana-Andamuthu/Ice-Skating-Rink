Getting to Frostiva Ice Arena

Centrally located in Arctic Ridge with direct access via Glacier Expressway, public metro links, and free heated parking.

By Car & Heated Parking

Take Exit 14 off Glacier Expressway and follow Glacier Avenue for 0.8 miles. Over 350 complimentary heated indoor parking bays with 12 Level-2 EV charging points are available for all guests.

Metro & Rapid Transit

Located a 3-minute walk from Arctic Ridge Central Metro Station (Blue Line). The Frostiva Heated Express Shuttle departs station Platform B every 15 minutes during public session peak times.

Accessibility & Entry

Step-free ramp access is available at the North Entrance. Heated spectator viewing mezzanine, family changing suites, and ADA-compliant rink transition zones are provided throughout the facility.

Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before visiting Frostiva Ice Skating Rink.

While walk-in tickets are available at our front desk ticketing counter, we strongly recommend reserving your slot online. Popular sessions—especially Friday Cosmic Glow Nights and Weekend Family Matinees—often reach maximum ice capacity. Online reservations guarantee your ice time with zero waiting.
Standard rental skates (Jackson figure skates & Bauer hockey skates) are included with all Unlimited Passes and VIP passes, and are available for just $4.50 with single public admission tickets. Toddler double-blade training skates, Bobby the Seal skate aids, and certified safety helmets are always provided free of charge.
The arena temperature is maintained at an optimal 55°F (13°C). We recommend comfortable athletic layers (stretchy joggers or leggings, a breathable fleece or light jacket), warm gloves, and a single pair of tall, snug athletic socks. Avoid thick double-socks, as they cause friction blisters inside fitted skates.
Birthday party packages should ideally be booked 2 to 3 weeks in advance to secure your preferred private party suite and prime session times. Full arena buyouts for corporate galas, ice hockey tournaments, or private film shoots require at least 30 days advance notice.
