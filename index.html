The Skater's Journey

From your very first hesitant step on ice to soaring across the arena with complete confidence.

First Fitting & Glide

Milestone 02

Edge & Stop Control

Master the snowplow stop, two-foot glide, and forward crossovers with encouraging coach pointers.

Milestone 03
