Stress-Free Planning

How Your Party Unfolds

Our dedicated party coordinators handle setup, skate fitting, and cleanup so parents can relax and enjoy.

1. VIP Arrival & Fit

Arrive 15 minutes before ice time. Your party host greets guests and assists with fast-track skate fitting.

2. On-Ice Excitement

Enjoy 75–90 minutes of supervised ice skating with fun games, music, and balance aids for young skaters.

3. Private Suite Feast

Gather in your private, warm decorated party room for hot pizza, drinks, and cake celebration.

4. Gifts & Souvenirs

Celebrate with gifts and send everyone home with commemorative Frostiva skate passes. Zero cleanup for you!
