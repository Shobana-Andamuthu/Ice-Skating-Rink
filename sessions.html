Pro Tips for First-Time Visitors

  • Socks: Bring mid-calf or knee-high athletic socks for maximum ankle support.
  • Layers: Wear breathable, flexible layers with light gloves and a warm jacket.
  • Kids Safety: Certified helmets and seal balance aids are 100% complimentary for under-12s.
  • Session Pacing: Fast outer lanes for fitness laps, safe interior slow zone for beginners.
  • Skate Marshals: CPR & first-aid certified marshals on ice for safety and beginner pointers.
  • RFID Lockers: Tap-to-lock smart keyless lockers included with every admission pass.
Skate rentals, protective helmets, and beginner aids can be pre-selected during online booking.
